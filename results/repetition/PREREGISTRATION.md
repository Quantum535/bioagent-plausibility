# Repetition versus consistency (registered before running)
Objection addressed: does "agreement" mean a field that is derivably consistent with the wrong value, or does any repetition of the number do?
Arms (60 compounds, weight asked, full record, plausible, three samples; models Opus 5, Argon, Flash, DeepSeek): R0 weight alone (0 agreeing);
R1 a verbatim repeat field (molecular_weight_reported) scaled with the weight (a repeat agrees); R2 the repeat left correct (a repeat
disagrees); B (existing) heavy-atom count scaled with the weight (a derived field agrees).
Predictions. RC1: R1 > R0 (a repeat raises relay). RC2: B > R1 by >= 10 points pooled over models (a derived agreeing field raises relay
more than a repeat). RC3: R2 < R0 (a disagreeing repeat lowers relay). Interpretation: RC1 and RC2 both met = agreement is weighted by
derivational content, with repetition contributing; RC1 met and RC2 not = repetition-weighting, and the paper's framing changes to
"consistency, including mere repetition"; RC1 not met = only derived agreement counts. Tests: one-sided McNemar per contrast, pooled.
