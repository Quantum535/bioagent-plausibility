\subsection*{Predictions that failed}

Several registered predictions failed, and Methods reports each with its test: fields with an exact derivation route were relayed more, not less, in deployment; monoisotopic-mass relay did not follow the molecular-weight boundaries, and the boundaries did not transfer to new compounds; the tool instruction mattered more than predicted; a repeated value did not act as agreement; Opus~5's override of contradicted lengths depended on identity; and the keyword detector registered for unprompted flags failed validation against human labels ($\kappa = 0.39$) and was replaced by a validated classifier.
