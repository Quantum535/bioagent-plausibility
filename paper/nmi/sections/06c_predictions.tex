\subsection*{Registered predictions}

Supplementary Table~21 lists all 48 registered predictions with their tests and outcomes: 20 held, 17 failed, 10 held in part and 1 could not be tested. The failures include that fields with an exact derivation route would be protected in deployment, that monoisotopic-mass relay would follow the molecular-weight boundaries, that the instruction to use the tools would not matter, that a repeated value would act as agreement, that Opus~5's override would not depend on the protein's identity, and that the registered keyword detector for unprompted doubt would pass validation against human labels ($\kappa = 0.39$; it was replaced by a classifier validated before use).
